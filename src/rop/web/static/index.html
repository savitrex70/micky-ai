Micky AI

A local-first personal assistant. Runs on your machine.

Early preview

Only the help capability is connected, and it changes nothing. Every request is checked against a local authorization policy before a capability may answer, so a request that is unclear, needs confirmation, or is not permitted is stopped instead of run.

Your request text and this tab's history are transient unless you have selected a saved conversation: only a successful exchange in a selected conversation is saved locally, and only after the server confirms it. Confirmation records are a separate local store with their own thirty-day retention and redaction rules; they are available through the confirmation-records section below and can be inspected, exported, and deleted. Conversations, confirmation records, and clinical reasoning data are kept in separate application stores. Notes, tasks, and reminders are not persisted.

Saved conversations

A successful exchange is saved when you have selected a conversation; you can reopen it after a refresh. Requests without a selected conversation stay transient. Saved conversations are kept separately from confirmation records and from clinical reasoning data; they are not clinical records.
